About CoreSafe Compliance

CoreSafe Compliance was created to provide businesses with straightforward, practical health, safety and environmental support without unnecessary jargon or complicated paperwork.

I have experience working across health, safety, environmental and compliance management, developing practical documentation and systems for real working environments.

As a NEBOSH and IOSH qualified health and safety professional, I understand that compliance documentation needs to do more than sit in a folder — it needs to reflect how your business actually operates and be something your employees can understand and use.

My approach is simple: practical advice, clear documentation and solutions that work for your business.
